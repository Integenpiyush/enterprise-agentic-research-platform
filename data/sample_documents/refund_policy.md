# Northstar Analytics Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Synthetic demonstration document.** Northstar Analytics is a fictional company; this policy is invented for this project.

Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days of the original purchase for an unused annual subscription. Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time, but charges already processed for the current billing month are not refundable. If a service outage caused by Northstar lasts more than 24 consecutive hours, affected customers may request a prorated service credit. Refund requests must be submitted to billing@example.invalid with the order number. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Taxes are refunded where required by applicable law.
