# Atlas Analytics Product Guide

**Synthetic demonstration document.** Atlas Analytics is a fictional product.

Atlas Analytics provides dashboards for revenue trends, customer cohorts, and product adoption. The standard data refresh runs every 24 hours. Workspace administrators can create up to 20 dashboards and share them with role-based access. CSV export is available to Analyst and Administrator roles. The product supports single sign-on on Enterprise plans. The guide recommends comparing at least two complete periods before interpreting a trend and labeling forecasts separately from observed results.
